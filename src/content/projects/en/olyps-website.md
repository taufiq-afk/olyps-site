### Goal

To give our work a clear, honest home that readers can verify.

### Scope

Research, projects, publications, people, and contact, in English and Indonesian, with room for new topics and collaborators over time.

### **Principles**

Every project carries a status label, such as Exploratory, Ongoing, or Published. Results appear only when they can be traced to a paper, a repository, or a dataset. Every page is written in both languages.
