## Question or hypothesis

Can object detection help estimate how much of a recovered paper stream is contamination, so that sorting decisions can be made earlier?

## Methods

Transformer-based object detection, such as RF-DETR, applied to images of recovered paper. Contamination is considered by weight rather than by item count. The setup and evaluation will be documented here as they are defined.

## Findings

No findings have been published yet.
