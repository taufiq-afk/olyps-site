## Question or hypothesis

How can speech and text be combined to recognize emotion more reliably than either alone?

## Methods

Text models such as BERT and speech models such as Wav2Vec2, evaluated on the public datasets GoEmotions, RAVDESS, and MELD, with multimodal fusion as the next step.

No findings have been published yet.
